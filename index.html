ABOUT THE STUDIO

Thoughtful Design. Timeless Living.

Founded with a conviction that our surroundings profoundly shape our sense of peace, AURELLE Interiors crafts refined spaces that transcend fleeting trends.

We collaborate closely with private homeowners, discerning collectors, and architectural partners across the UK and mainland Europe. From historic country estates to sleek metropolitan penthouses, our multidisciplinary studio balances structural rigor with tactile warmth.

Our method begins not with furniture catalogues, but with light, proportion, and human habit. By curating raw lime-washed plaster, honed limestone, seasoned oak, and custom upholstery, we craft homes imbued with effortless calm and enduring character.

DESIGN MANIFESTO

“Less, But Better.”

We believe an extraordinary interior does not rely on visual noise or fleeting fashion. True elegance is found in the restraint of subtraction—allowing pure proportions, natural sunlight, and honest materiality to take center stage.

Tactile Honesty

We favor surfaces that feel genuine to the touch: untreated linen, open-grain oak, unlacquered bronze, and honed stone that acquire beauty with age.

Daylight as Architecture

Natural illumination dictates our spatial layout. We study how dawn and dusk wash across every room, molding atmospheric warmth without harsh shadows.

Timeless Longevity

By resisting disposable cycles and prioritizing custom artisan joinery, our spaces are designed to remain compelling for generations.

— AURELLE Studio Principles, Vol. IV
